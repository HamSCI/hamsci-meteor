## Mission
{:.no_toc}

The HamSCI Meteor Scatter Working Group coordinates the efforts of amateur radio operators, citizen scientists, and professional researchers to use meteor scatter observations for the advancement of meteor science and meteor scatter communications. Through activities such as the Meteor Scatter QSO Party and citizen science data collection and analysis, the group promotes meteor scatter operations while contributing to scientific discovery and communications research.

*Draft mission statement, presented at the kickoff meeting on 9 September 2026.*

## Part of HamSCI

The working group operates under [HamSCI](https://hamsci.org) (Ham Radio Science Citizen Investigation), whose three goals it shares:

- Advance scientific research and understanding through amateur radio activities.
- Encourage the development of new technologies to support this research.
- Provide educational opportunities for the amateur community and the general public.

The working group organizes its own goals into [science, communications engineering, and ham radio goals]({{ '/goals/' | relative_url }}).
