Welcome to the HamSCI Meteor Scatter Working Group. The group brings together amateur radio operators, citizen scientists, and professional researchers who use meteor scatter observations to study meteors and to improve meteor scatter communications. Its main activity is the [Meteor Scatter QSO Party (MSQP)]({{ '/msqp/' | relative_url }}), in which operators work meteor scatter during major meteor showers and archive their recordings for analysis.

The working group held its kickoff meeting on 9 September 2026 and meets weekly on Zoom. See [About]({{ '/about/' | relative_url }}) for how to join.

**Next MSQP target shower:** the Geminids, 13--15 December 2026.

If you have any questions or suggestions, please [**open a new issue**](https://github.com/HamSCI/hamsci-meteor/issues/new). This leaves a public record that can help others.
