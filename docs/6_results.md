## Datasets

MSQP participants archive their recordings (MSK144 WAV files, and often their WSJT-X logs) on Zenodo, one dataset per station per shower, in the [HamSCI community](https://zenodo.org/communities/hamsci). Every dataset has its own DOI and is released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license.

**[Browse all Meteor Scatter QSO Party datasets on Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/communities/hamsci/records?q=%22Meteor%20Scatter%20QSO%20Party%22)**

Dataset titles follow the pattern *callsign, grid square, dates operated, "Meteor Scatter QSO Party"*, so a station's recordings can be found by searching for its callsign. When you use a dataset, cite it by its DOI.
