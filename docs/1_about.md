The HamSCI Meteor Scatter Working Group coordinates amateur radio operators, citizen scientists, and professional researchers who use meteor scatter observations for meteor science and communications research. It builds on the [Meteor Scatter QSO Party]({{ '/msqp/' | relative_url }}), which HamSCI has run during major meteor showers since the 2025 Perseids, and held its kickoff meeting on 9 September 2026.

## Meetings

The working group meets weekly on Zoom and may move to every other week later. Meeting links and announcements go out on the mailing list. Minutes and action items are kept on the [Meeting Minutes]({{ '/minutes/' | relative_url }}) page.

## How to join

- **Mailing list:** HamSCI-Meteor@scranton.edu, hosted by the University of Scranton (being set up as of the kickoff meeting).
- **Take part in the next MSQP.** Everyone is welcome to listen, make contacts, and upload recordings. See the [MSQP page]({{ '/msqp/' | relative_url }}).
- **Help with analysis.** The group needs volunteers to develop analysis scripts and run them over the MSQP recordings. See [Goals]({{ '/goals/' | relative_url }}) for the open problems.
- **Ask a question** by [opening an issue](https://github.com/HamSCI/hamsci-meteor/issues/new) on this site's repository.

## Related HamSCI working groups

- [Forward-Scatter Meteor Radar Network](https://www.hamsci.org/working-group/forward-scatter-meteor-radar-network), led by Chip Sufitchi (N2YO), is developing a GPS-disciplined forward-scatter radar network built around a low-power CW beacon near 50 MHz, for meteor trajectory reconstruction and radio meteor astronomy.
